Biography & Academic Journey

My journey began in Andracha Woreda, Sheka Zone, where I completed my elementary and primary education from Grade 1 through Grade 9. Demonstrating strong academic excellence, I was selected to join the prestigious Mizan-Tepi University Sheka Zone Special Boarding School, where I completed Grade 10 and am currently an 11th Grade student in the Natural Science stream.

In 2018 E.C., I officially joined the Mizan-Tepi University STEM Center, immersing myself in Electronics, Robotics, and Computer Programming—specifically mastering Python. Over time, I hands-on built hardware innovations including Fire-Fighting Robots and Obstacle-Avoiding Autonomous Robots.

In 2026, I participated in the prestigious AddisCoder Summer Program in Addis Ababa, sharpening my knowledge in advanced data structures, algorithms, and computational thinking. Alongside my deep commitment to Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Information & Communication Technology (ICT), I am actively training myself in Large Language Models (LLMs), AI architectures, and Machine Learning with the ultimate ambition of becoming a lead AI Engineer and Chip Designer.
